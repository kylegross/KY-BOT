# KY BOT — Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: October 7, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how KY BOT (“KY BOT,” “the Bot,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, processes, stores, and protects information when you interact with KY BOT through Discord.

Privacy is approached with a simple principle: **collect only what is reasonably necessary for KY BOT to function.**

By using KY BOT, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to information processed through your use of KY BOT, including its commands, interactive components, server configurations, automation features, and related services.

KY BOT operates within Discord and is not responsible for information independently collected or processed by Discord.

Your use of Discord remains subject to Discord’s own terms and privacy practices.

## 2. Information KY BOT May Process

The information processed by KY BOT depends on the features you use.

### Discord Identifiers

KY BOT may process identifiers provided through Discord, including:

- Discord user IDs
- Discord server or guild IDs
- Channel IDs
- Role IDs
- Message IDs
- Thread IDs
- Interaction and command identifiers

These identifiers allow KY BOT to determine where commands originate, apply configurations to the correct server or channel, manage permissions, and provide requested functionality.

### Server Configuration Data

When administrators configure KY BOT, the Bot may store information necessary to preserve those settings.

This may include:

- Enabled or disabled features
- Channel selections
- Role selections
- Command configurations
- Automation settings
- Server-specific preferences
- Feature permissions
- Scheduled or persistent configurations

This information allows server settings to remain available between interactions and Bot restarts.

### Command and Interaction Data

When you interact with KY BOT, certain information associated with that interaction may be processed to execute the requested command or feature.

This may include the command used, selections made through menus or buttons, submitted values, and other information required to complete the requested action.

### Message and Content Data

Certain features may require KY BOT to access or process Discord messages or other user-generated content.

For example, automation, message-management, moderation, game, thread-management, or similar features may require access to relevant content in order to perform their intended function.

Access to content does not necessarily mean that content is permanently stored.

Unless a feature specifically requires retention, KY BOT should process content only for the period reasonably necessary to perform the requested function.



### Server Activity Logs

When a server administrator selects a log channel, KY BOT posts message edits and
deletions, member joins and departures, role changes, bans and unbans, and invite
creation and deletion to that channel. Logs can contain user, channel, role and
message identifiers, recent cached message text, attachment filenames, and invite
codes. Who can view these logs depends on the channel permissions set by the server.

Recent message text and member information are held in memory to process events;
this feature does not store a message archive in the bot database. Log posts are
stored by Discord and remain until removed by server moderators. Clearing the
configured log channel stops future activity logging and does not remove prior posts.

### Technical and Diagnostic Information

KY BOT may maintain limited operational information necessary to diagnose errors, maintain security, prevent abuse, and improve service reliability.

This may include command errors, timestamps, feature usage information, system events, and related technical information.

## 3. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

Unless required for a clearly identified feature, KY BOT is not designed to intentionally collect:

- Passwords or Discord login credentials
- Payment card information
- Government identification numbers
- Precise physical location
- Private messages unrelated to Bot functionality
- Sensitive personal information that is unnecessary for operation of the Bot

You should not submit sensitive personal information to KY BOT unless a specific feature expressly requires that information and explains how it will be handled.

## 4. How Information Is Used

Information processed by KY BOT may be used to:

- Execute commands and interactions.
- Maintain server-specific configurations.
- Provide automation and management functionality.
- Determine applicable permissions.
- Preserve user or server preferences.
- Operate games, utilities, and interactive features.
- Diagnose technical issues.
- Maintain the security and reliability of the Bot.
- Prevent abuse, exploitation, or unauthorized use.
- Develop, maintain, and improve KY BOT.
- Comply with applicable legal obligations.

Information will not be used for purposes materially unrelated to operating KY BOT without appropriate notice where required.

## 5. Data Minimization

KY BOT is designed around data minimization.

Where practical, information is processed temporarily rather than permanently stored. Persistent information should be limited to what is reasonably necessary to maintain configurations, provide requested functionality, ensure security, and operate the service.

The mere ability of KY BOT to access information through Discord permissions does not mean that all accessible information is collected or retained.

## 6. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, subject to operational, security, and legal requirements.

Server configuration information may be retained while KY BOT remains configured for a server.

Certain information may be removed when:

- A configuration is deleted.
- A feature is disabled.
- KY BOT is removed from a server.
- The information is no longer required to provide the applicable functionality.
- A valid deletion request is completed.

Limited technical or security records may be retained for a reasonable period when necessary to investigate errors, prevent abuse, protect the service, or comply with legal obligations.

Backup systems may temporarily retain deleted information until the applicable backup is overwritten or expires.

## 7. Data Sharing

KY BOT does **not sell personal information.**

Information may be disclosed only where reasonably necessary to operate or protect the service, including to infrastructure or service providers that support KY BOT.

Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to:

- Comply with applicable law, regulation, legal process, or lawful governmental request.
- Protect the security or integrity of KY BOT.
- Investigate fraud, abuse, exploitation, or security incidents.
- Protect the rights or safety of KY BOT, its users, operators, or others.
- Facilitate a business transfer, restructuring, or change in ownership, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Third-party service providers may process limited information on behalf of KY BOT where necessary to provide hosting, databases, monitoring, or other infrastructure.

## 8. Third-Party Services

KY BOT operates primarily through Discord and may rely on additional third-party infrastructure or APIs.

These services operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

KY BOT cannot control how Discord or independent third-party providers collect, process, secure, or retain information through their own services.

## 9. Permissions

KY BOT may request Discord permissions necessary to provide particular features.

Server administrators control which permissions are granted to the Bot.

Some functionality may not operate correctly when required permissions are unavailable.

Permissions granted to KY BOT should not be interpreted as evidence that every category of information accessible through those permissions is collected or permanently stored.

## 10. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information processed by KY BOT against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss, or misuse.

However, no online service, database, or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

Users and server administrators should also maintain appropriate Discord permissions and security practices.

### Checklist Content and History

When an administrator creates a checklist, authorized messages in its channel are
saved as tasks. The database retains task text, author/source identifiers, attachment
metadata, categories, priorities, custom artwork, thread identifiers and completion
history. Source messages are removed only after the board updates successfully and
attachments are re-uploaded into a private task discussion thread.

Deleting a task removes it from the visible checklist but retains its saved record,
discussion and completion history. Checklist logs are optional: choosing Yes opens a
server channel picker, while No disables delivery. Completions made with logs off
are not posted later. History remains in the checklist database. Existing Discord
log posts and discussion threads remain subject to server/channel permissions and
moderator removal.
